Questions

Do you keep logs?

No activity logs. While you are connected, the server keeps only your device's anonymous VPN key, its purchase ID and the time your session ends, so it can route your traffic. Those are deleted when the session ends.

Is the free version really free?

Yes. Free sessions last 60 minutes on the free locations, at up to 20 Mbit/s with a fair-use data allowance per session; reconnect for another session. Pro removes the limits and unlocks every location in 19 countries.

Does Haven block ads in other apps?

No. The VPN never shows, blocks or changes ads in other apps. Shield blocks known malware and phishing sites only.

How do I cancel?

Open Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Haven VPN → Cancel.
