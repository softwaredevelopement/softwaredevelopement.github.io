Subscription help

I paid but the app still shows ads or locked features

Open the app, go to Settings and use Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed into Google Play with the same account you bought with. If it still does not unlock, email us with the Google Play order number from your receipt.

How do I cancel?

In the Play Store: your profile picture › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › Haven VPN › Cancel subscription. You keep premium until the end of the period you already paid for. Uninstalling the app does not cancel it.

I want a refund

Refunds are issued by Google Play, not by us. Request one at play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory within 48 hours of purchase, or email us and we will ask Google on your behalf.

Common questions

Do you keep logs?

No activity logs. While you are connected, the server keeps only your device's anonymous VPN key, its purchase ID and the time your session ends, so it can route your traffic. Those are deleted when the session ends.

Is the free version really free?

Yes. Free sessions last 60 minutes on the free locations; reconnect for another session. Pro removes the limit and unlocks every location.

Does Haven block ads in other apps?

No. The VPN never shows, blocks or changes ads in other apps. Shield blocks known malware and phishing sites only.

How do I cancel?

Open Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Haven VPN → Cancel.
