Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 16, 2026  |  Last Updated: September 16, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Pro PDF Editor, developed by Pivot Master Apps. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our Android application.

We designed Pro PDF Editor with privacy as a core principle: all document rendering, editing, conversion, and encryption is performed entirely on your device. Your PDF files and their contents are never uploaded to our servers.

By using Pro PDF Editor, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the app.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Do Not Provide — On-Device Processing Only

Pro PDF Editor does not require you to create an account. The app reads the documents you open in order to display, edit, annotate, sign, merge, split, compress, convert, or encrypt them. All of this processing happens entirely on your device, and neither your documents nor the text, images, form data, or signatures inside them are transmitted to us or to any third party.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

To improve app performance and diagnose issues, we may collect limited technical data through third-party SDKs:

  • Crash reports — anonymous crash logs (stack traces, device model, OS version) to help us fix bugs
  • Usage analytics — anonymous events such as which tools are used and how often (no file names, paths, or document content are included)
  • Device information — device model, OS version, app version, and screen resolution

2.3 Purchase Information

If you upgrade to Pro PDF Editor Premium, your purchase is processed entirely through Google Play. We receive confirmation of your purchase status but do not receive or store your payment card details.

2.4 Advertising

The free version of the app displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use data (such as a device advertising ID) to serve personalized or contextual ads. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details on how AdMob handles data.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the app's core features
  • To diagnose crashes and fix technical issues
  • To understand feature usage so we can improve the app
  • To serve advertisements to free-tier users
  • To verify premium subscription status
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not use your data for profiling, sell your data to third parties, or use it for any purpose beyond operating and improving the app.

4. Document & File Access

Pro PDF Editor requests access to documents on your device so it can open and save PDF files. Depending on your Android version, this is granted either through the system file picker (Storage Access Framework) or through a storage permission. This access is the core function of the app.

Important: We never upload, copy, or transmit your PDFs, their text, embedded images, form entries, or signatures to external servers. All rendering, editing, merging, compression, conversion, and encryption is done locally on your device.

When you save changes, export a new file, or delete a document through the app, the operation is performed by the Android system on your device's own storage. Temporary working files the app creates during an operation are written to the app's private cache and removed when the operation completes or when you clear the app's cache.

4.1 Passwords You Enter

If you open a password-protected PDF or apply password protection to one, the password is used in memory on your device solely to decrypt or encrypt that document. We do not store, log, or transmit document passwords, and we have no ability to recover a password you have lost.

5. Third-Party Services

Pro PDF Editor integrates the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics (Google) — anonymous analytics and crash reporting. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob — advertisement delivery for free-tier users. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing — in-app purchase and subscription management. Google Play Terms

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party services.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the limited data we collect:

  • All data transmitted to third-party services uses HTTPS encryption
  • Crash reports and analytics are anonymized before transmission
  • No documents, document content, or document passwords ever leave your device

However, no method of data transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted over the internet.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers — third-party SDKs listed in Section 5, solely to operate the app
  • Legal requirements — if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of our users
  • Business transfers — in the event of a merger or acquisition, subject to the same privacy protections

8. Your Rights & Choices

8.1 File Access Permission

You can revoke Pro PDF Editor's file access at any time in your device Settings → Apps → Pro PDF Editor → Permissions. Without file access the core viewing and editing features will not function.

8.2 Ad Personalization

You can opt out of personalized ads by resetting or opting out of your device's advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.

8.3 Analytics Opt-Out

Firebase Analytics respects the device-level "Opt out of Ads Personalization" setting. You can also disable analytics collection by contacting us at pivotmasterapps@gmail.com.

8.4 Data Deletion

Since we do not collect personally identifiable information tied to your identity, there is minimal personal data to delete. If you have concerns, contact us and we will work with you to address them. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored app data, including its recent-documents list and cache, from your device.

9. Children's Privacy

Pro PDF Editor is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

10. International Data Transfers

Our third-party service providers (Google Firebase, AdMob) may process data in countries outside your own, including the United States. By using Pro PDF Editor, you consent to your data being transferred to and processed in these countries, which may have different data protection laws than your jurisdiction.

11. GDPR Rights (EU / EEA Users)

If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right to access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to restrict processing — request that we limit how we use your data
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interests

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at pivotmasterapps@gmail.com.

12. CCPA Rights (California Residents)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to know — what personal information we collect and how it is used
  • Right to delete — request deletion of personal information we have collected
  • Right to opt out — opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information)
  • Right to non-discrimination — we will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights

To submit a CCPA request, contact us at pivotmasterapps@gmail.com.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top. For material changes, we will notify you via an in-app notice. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
