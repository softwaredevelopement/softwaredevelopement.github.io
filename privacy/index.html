Privacy Policy

Pivot Master Apps — Company-Wide Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026  |  Last Updated: March 1, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Pivot Master Apps ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our websites, Android mobile applications, and related services (collectively, the "Services"). This policy applies to all products and services offered by Pivot Master Apps, including TravelMate, Storage Cleaner, and KøbSmart.

By using any of our Services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data responsibly.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account registration details (e.g., email address) where accounts are supported
  • User preferences and settings within our apps
  • Communications you send us (support requests, feedback)

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device identifiers (Android Advertising ID) for advertising and analytics
  • App usage data (session duration, feature usage) via Firebase Analytics
  • Crash and performance data via Firebase Crashlytics
  • IP address and general location (country/region level) for analytics
  • App version, OS version, and device model for diagnostics

2.3 Information Processed On-Device

Certain apps (particularly Storage Cleaner) process files, photos, and device data entirely on your device. This data is never uploaded to our servers or any third-party server.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our applications and services
  • To display relevant advertisements via Google AdMob and other ad networks
  • To run and optimize Google App Campaigns that promote our apps via the Google Ads API
  • To analyze app performance and user behavior to improve features
  • To diagnose crashes and fix technical issues
  • To respond to support requests and communications
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations

4. Google Ads and Advertising

We use the Google Ads API to manage promotional campaigns for our Android applications. These campaigns run on Google Search, Google Play Store, YouTube, and the Google Display Network, and are designed to acquire new users for our apps. The API is used for campaign creation, bid management, budget control, and performance reporting.

Our apps display in-app advertising through Google AdMob (Publisher ID: pub-2159500504641406). AdMob may use your device's Advertising ID to serve personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your Android device settings under Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization.

We also work with Meta Audience Network and AppLovin for additional in-app advertising. Each network's privacy policy governs their data use.

5. Third-Party Services

Our Services integrate with the following third-party providers:

  • Google Firebase — Analytics, Crashlytics, Authentication, and Cloud Storage
  • Google AdMob — In-app advertising
  • Google Ads — App promotion campaigns via the Google Ads API
  • Google Maps — Mapping and location features (TravelMate)
  • Meta Audience Network — In-app advertising
  • AppLovin — In-app advertising
  • Google Play Billing — In-app purchases and subscriptions
  • Claude API / Google Gemini — AI-powered features (TravelMate)

Each third party operates under its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review those policies.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Third-party service providers listed in Section 5, to the extent necessary to operate our Services
  • Law enforcement or government authorities, when required by applicable law
  • Successor entities in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. Firebase Analytics data is retained for 14 months. Crash data is retained for 90 days. Upon account deletion, personal data is removed within 30 days.

8. Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures to protect your information, including HTTPS encryption, Firebase security rules, and access controls. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access — Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you

  • Deletion — Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")

  • Objection — Object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at pivotmasterapps@gmail.com.
