Support

Effective September 25, 2026  |  Last updated September 25, 2026

Get help

Questions, a problem, or an idea for the game? Write to pivotmasterapps@gmail.com - we answer within two working days. Please include your phone model and the app version (Settings › App version in the grown-ups' area).

Common questions

How do I reach the settings?

Tap the gear on the map and answer the grown-ups' question.

Can I change what my child practises?

Yes: grown-ups' area › Edit setup. The game also adapts by itself after every answer.

The questions aren't read aloud.

Read-aloud uses the phone's text-to-speech voice. Make sure it's switched on in the setup and that a voice for your language is installed (Android Settings › Accessibility › Text-to-speech).

How do I cancel the subscription?

Google Play › Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › Math Knight.

Delete your data

Math Knight by Pivot Master Apps has no accounts. What it knows about your child - the optional first name, age, chosen skills, progress, stars and settings - is stored only on the phone.

  • To delete it: Android Settings › Apps › Math Knight › Storage › Clear storage, or uninstall the app. It is deleted immediately and completely.
  • Anonymous analytics and crash reports (no name, age or answers) are kept by Google Firebase for up to 14 months (analytics) and 90 days (crash reports), then deleted automatically. To have them deleted sooner, email pivotmasterapps@gmail.com with the subject "Delete Math Knight data"; we act within 30 days.
  • Subscriptions are held by Google Play; cancel them in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions.
