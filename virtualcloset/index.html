Virtual Closet

Your wardrobe, digitised.

Photograph a piece and it is cut out of its background and filed in your closet. Put outfits together from what you actually own, arrange them the way you would lay them on a bed, and plan what to wear.

Your wardrobe lives on your phone. Photos are sent to our server only when you ask for a cutout or a 3D pass, and they are not stored there.

What it does

  • Cutouts. A photo of a garment becomes a clean cutout with the background removed.
  • 3D. A flat photo can be redrawn so it looks worn, with volume and drape.
  • Looks. Build outfits from tops, bottoms, shoes and anything else, arranged how you like.
  • Planning. Put a look on a day and see the week ahead.
