2. Description of Service

Pro PDF Editor is an Android application that helps you:

  • View and search PDF documents
  • Edit text and images inside existing PDF pages
  • Annotate, highlight, and mark up documents
  • Fill in forms and add a handwritten or imported signature
  • Merge, split, reorder, rotate, and delete pages
  • Compress documents and convert between PDFs and images
  • Add or remove password protection on a document

All document processing is performed locally on your device. The App does not upload your documents to any external server.

Certain advanced features require a Pro PDF Editor Premium subscription, available through Google Play.
