Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026  |  Last updated September 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Math Knight ("the app") is a maths adventure game for children, published on Google Play by Pivot Master Apps ("we", "us") under the package name com.pivotmasterapps.mathknight. This policy explains what information the app handles, what leaves the device and what does not, and the choices parents have.

No ads. No advertising ID. No accounts. Nothing about your child leaves the phone.

2. What the app collects

"Collects" means information that leaves the device. Everything else happens only on the phone and is described in section 3.

InformationWhenWhyWhere it goes
App activity (anonymous)While the app is usedTo see which parts of the game are used and where players get stuckGoogle Firebase Analytics
Crash logs and diagnosticsWhen the app crashesTo find and fix crashesGoogle Firebase Crashlytics
Purchase statusWhen a parent subscribesTo unlock the full gameGoogle Play

Analytics events are anonymous: they carry an app-instance identifier, the device model, Android and app version and coarse region - never the child's name, age or answers. The app turns off Google Analytics' advertising features (no ad-personalisation signals, no advertising-ID collection).

What the app never collects

  • The child's name, age or anything typed into the app
  • Answers, scores, stars or progress
  • The advertising ID (the app removes the permission for it)
  • Name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location
  • Voice or audio of any kind - the app never uses the microphone

3. What stays on the device

The optional hero name, the child's age, the skills chosen for practice, game progress, stars, unlocked characters and settings (music, sounds, read-aloud, relaxed mode, language) are saved only in the app's private storage on the phone. They are never uploaded and are removed when the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared.

Read-aloud uses the phone's own text-to-speech engine to speak questions for children who cannot read yet. The app hands the engine only the sentence to be spoken; how the engine works is set in the phone's own settings.

4. Advertising

The app shows no advertising of any kind and does not use the advertising ID.

5. Purchases and the parental gate

The first kingdom is free. The full game is unlocked with a subscription sold through Google Play; we never see or store payment details. Buying, managing subscriptions and following links out of the app are placed behind a parental gate - a question a young child cannot answer - so that purchases are made by a grown-up.

6. Children

Math Knight is designed for children, and we treat everyone who uses it as a child. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children: the app has no accounts, no chat, no user-generated content shared with others, no ads and no advertising ID, and the personal details a parent enters (such as a first name) never leave the device. The analytics and crash reporting described in section 2 are anonymous, used only to run and improve the app, and never used for advertising or profiling. This is how we meet the requirements of the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR (including its rules for children) and Google Play's Families policy.

A parent can clear everything the app stored at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it, and can contact us about anything in this policy.

7. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config) - anonymous usage analytics, crash reports and remote configuration (privacy policy)
  • Google Play Billing - processes purchases (privacy policy)

We do not sell information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own purposes.

8. Retention and security

Analytics events are kept for 14 months (the Google Analytics default) and crash reports for 90 days. Everything the app saves on the phone stays there until the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared. All communication with these services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

9. Your rights

You may ask us for access to, correction of, or deletion of any information we hold. Because the app keeps personal details only on the device, uninstalling it deletes them. If you are in the EEA or the UK you may also complain to your local data protection authority. Write to pivotmasterapps@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days.

10. International transfers

Google operates globally, so anonymous analytics and crash reports may be processed outside your country, including in the United States, under Google's standard contractual clauses and equivalent safeguards.

11. Changes to this policy

We update this policy whenever the app changes what it does with information, and change the "last updated" date above.
